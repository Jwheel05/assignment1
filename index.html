About this visualization

Dataset: CMSC471's Daily Weather in the U.S., 2017, sourced from NOAA GHCN. States and actual station names come directly from the original file, including Puerto Rico and Guam.

Design: Two station-specific temperature lines and grouped precipitation bars support comparison. State and station dropdowns filter records; hover reveals details; brushing the temperature chart filters precipitation totals.

Data handling: We retain all available stations. Duplicate readings for the same state, station and date are averaged per measurement to avoid counting precipitation twice. Missing measurements are excluded. The supplied values are Fahrenheit and inches.
