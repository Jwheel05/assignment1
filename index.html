About this visualization

Dataset: CMSC471's Daily Weather in the U.S., 2017, sourced from NOAA GHCN. States and actual station names come directly from the original file, including Puerto Rico and Guam.

Design: Two station-specific temperature lines and grouped precipitation/snow bars support comparison. State and station dropdowns filter records; hover reveals details; brushing the temperature chart zooms into dates and filters the lower chart.

Data handling: The supplied measurements use Fahrenheit and inches. Daily average temperature is calculated from minimum and maximum temperatures. Precipitation and snowfall are summed over each period; snow depth is averaged over available readings because it measures snow on the ground rather than new snowfall. Missing values are excluded, not treated as zero.
